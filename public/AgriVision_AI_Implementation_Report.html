3. 21-Disease Class Inventory and Clinical Treatments

Crop & Pathology Causal Pathogen Key Foliar Symptoms Recommended Treatment Protocol
Tomato: Early Blight Alternaria solani Concentric brown target rings; yellow margins. Prune lower foliage; drip irrigate; apply Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2g/L.
Tomato: Late Blight Phytophthora infestans Water-soaked greasy foliar collapse; abaxial mold. Rogue infected vines; spray Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2.5g/L.
Tomato: Bacterial Spot Xanthomonas perforans Angular dark spots with chlorotic halo; shot-hole. Copper hydroxide + mancozeb; sanitize pruning shears.
Tomato: Leaf Mold Passalora fulva Pale yellow chlorosis adaxially; olive mold below. Improve greenhouse ventilation; avoid wetting leaves.
Tomato: Septoria Spot Septoria lycopersici Circular spots with grey center & dark rims. Remove basal leaves; mulch soil; chlorothalonil application.
Tomato: Spider Mites Tetranychus urticae Chlorotic stippling, leaf bronzing, webbing. Neem oil spray (5%); predatory mite release.
Tomato: Target Spot Corynespora cassiicola Zonate necrotic lesions; pinhole tears. Increase row spacing; prophylactic azoxystrobin spray.
Tomato: Leaf Curl TYLCV (Begomovirus) Upward cupping; stunted bushy dwarfism. Yellow sticky traps for whitefly control; rogue infected vines.
Tomato: Mosaic Virus ToMV (Tobamovirus) Mottled light/dark green mosaic mottle. No cure; sanitize hands/tools; plant certified clean seeds.
Tomato: Healthy None (Pristine) Uniform green lamina; intact serrate margins. Maintain balanced N-P-K nutrition; periodic scout monitoring.
Potato: Early Blight Alternaria solani Dark brown angular spots with concentric rings. Avoid overhead watering; spray propiconazole or chlorothalonil.
Potato: Late Blight Phytophthora infestans Rapid purplish-black water-soaked foliar collapse. Bordeaux mixture 1% or cymoxanil spray; destroy cull piles.
Potato: Healthy None (Pristine) Vigorous chlorophyll density; sturdy compound foliage. Ensure well-drained soil; mound tubers to prevent exposure.
Chilli: Bacterial Spot Xanthomonas vesicatoria Small dark water-soaked spots; defoliation. Streptocycline (100 ppm) + copper oxychloride (0.2%).
Chilli: Cercospora Cercospora capsici Frog-eye spots with bleached white centers. Carbendazim 0.1% or mancozeb 0.2% foliar application.
Chilli: Leaf Curl ChiLCV (Begomovirus) Upward rolling of leaves; shortened internodes. Control thrips & whiteflies with imidacloprid; rogue plants.
Chilli: Powdery Mildew Leveillula taurica White powdery patches on leaf undersides. Wettable sulfur (0.2%) or dinocap spray at first symptom.
Chilli: Anthracnose Colletotrichum capsici Sunken circular spots on foliage and fruit. Seed treatment with Trichoderma; spray difenoconazole.
Chilli: Deficiency N/P/K/Mg deficiency Interveinal chlorosis, marginal purpling. Soil test guided micronutrient foliar spray (19-19-19 soluble).
Chilli: Others Unclassified / Mixed Atypical lesions or mechanical foliar abrasions. Resubmit clear photo; consult local Krishi Bhavan agronomist.
Chilli: Healthy None (Pristine) Glossy dark green lanceolate foliage. Regular irrigation at flowering; balanced organic compost.
